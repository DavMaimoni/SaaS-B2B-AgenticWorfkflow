title: Refund policy
tags: billing, refunds
---

- Duplicate or erroneous charges are always refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after confirmation by the billing team.
- Annual plans: a pro-rata refund of the unused months is possible if the cancellation is requested within 30 days after the renewal date.
- Monthly plans are not refundable; the subscription stays active until the end of the paid month.
Every refund is reviewed and approved by the billing team: support agents never promise a refund amount.
