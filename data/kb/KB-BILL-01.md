title: Plans and pricing
tags: billing, plans
---

Fluxo has three plans.
- Free: up to 5 users, 3 projects, community support.
- Pro: 12 USD per user per month billed monthly, or 10 USD per user per month billed annually. Unlimited projects, custom fields, integrations, email support.
- Enterprise: custom pricing (contact sales). Adds SSO (SAML), SCIM provisioning, IP allowlist, audit log, a 99.9 % uptime SLA and a named customer success manager.
Admins change the plan in Settings > Billing > Plan. Upgrades apply immediately and are billed pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
