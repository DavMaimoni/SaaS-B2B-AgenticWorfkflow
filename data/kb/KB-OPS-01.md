title: Outages, maintenance and status page
tags: outages, status
---

Live service status, incidents and maintenance are published on status.fluxo.example; subscribe there by email, SMS or RSS.
Planned maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance to admins and usually causes no downtime.
During an incident, support confirms what the status page shows and never gives a resolution time that is not published there. Enterprise customers are covered by a 99.9 % monthly uptime SLA with service credits described in their contract.
