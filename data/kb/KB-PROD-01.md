title: Projects, templates and views
tags: product
---

Save any project as a template from the project menu > Save as template; templates are shared with the whole workspace.
Views: list, board, Gantt and calendar. A read-only public link to any view can be created from Share > Public link, protected with a password and revoked at any time.
Custom fields (text, number, date, dropdown) are available on Pro and Enterprise in Project settings > Fields. Recurring tasks are set from the due date picker (daily, weekly, monthly).
