id: KB-INT-01
title: Integrations and webhooks
tags: integrations, technical
---

Integrations (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Drive, Salesforce, HubSpot) are managed in Settings > Integrations. If an integration stops syncing, disconnect and reconnect it, and check that the connected account still has access (for Slack, that the app is still invited to the channel).
Webhooks are configured in Settings > Integrations > Webhooks. A webhook is disabled automatically after 10 consecutive failed deliveries; re-enable it and use Send test event to check the endpoint.
