title: Failed payments and payment methods
tags: billing, payments
---

Accepted payment methods: credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) for all plans, bank transfer for annual plans above 5,000 USD.
When a payment fails, Fluxo retries for 7 days (grace period) and emails the billing contact. After the grace period the workspace becomes read-only until the card is updated in Settings > Billing > Payment method; access is restored as soon as the payment succeeds.
