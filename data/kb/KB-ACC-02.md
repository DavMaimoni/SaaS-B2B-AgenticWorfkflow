id: KB-ACC-02
title: Account deletion and data retention
tags: account, privacy
---

An admin requests deletion in Settings > Workspace > Delete workspace. The workspace is deactivated immediately and all data is permanently deleted after 30 days. Export your data first (Export > Full workspace export).
Fluxo hosts EU workspaces in data centers located in the European Union. The data processing agreement (DPA) can be reviewed and signed online in Settings > Legal.
