title: Invoices and company details
tags: billing, invoices
---

Every invoice can be downloaded as PDF in Settings > Billing > Invoices (admins and billing contacts only).
Company name, address and VAT number are edited in Settings > Billing > Company details; the change applies to invoices issued afterwards. Support can reissue a past invoice on request.
Invoices are sent by email to the billing contact on each renewal.
