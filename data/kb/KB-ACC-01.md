title: Members, seats and ownership
tags: account, members
---

Admins invite members in Settings > Members and can resend an invitation from the same page. Invitation emails come from no-reply@fluxo.example; ask the invitee to check spam filters.
Seats are added in Settings > Billing > Seats and billed pro rata for the current period.
Workspace ownership is transferred by an admin in Settings > Members. If no admin is reachable, support verifies the request from a company email address before transferring ownership.
